SETUP

The first launch walks you through seven short steps. Every one is optional and can be changed later in Settings (the gear on Home).

  1. Apple Health. Lets RepWarrior read your workouts, heart rate, sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, steps, and energy. Decline it and you still have the full strength logger and its coaching.
  2. Rest timer alerts. The one notification the app ever sends: the rest timer telling you your next set is up.
  3. Reading history. With Health access, up to 24 months of past workouts come in, so your fitness curve starts from where your training really is.
  4. Your numbers. Max heart rate, threshold heart rate, and FTP. Leave them alone and they sharpen from your own efforts. Type one in only if you know it from a test.
  5. Step quest. Pick a daily step target, or skip it.
  6. Coaching style. Choose whether you want a weekly nudge toward a hard session, and how often you aim to train each muscle group.
  7. Your week. Build a weekly plan now, or start without one.

LOGGING A WORKOUT

Everything starts on the LIFT tab. Tap the play button on a template, or Start empty workout and add exercises as you go. Four starter routines are there on day one, and you can make as many templates as you like with New template.

Logging a set

  • Each row shows last session's weight and reps in gray. Tap the circle to complete the set with those numbers, or type new ones first.
  • RIR is reps in reserve: how many more clean reps you had left. Logging it makes your load score more accurate.
  • W marks a warm-up set and F a set taken to failure.
  • The blue plate button beside the weight opens the plate calculator.
  • Timed holds like a plank get a stopwatch, and bodyweight moves drop the weight field.

During the session

  • The exercise menu (the … button) sets the rest timer and groups an exercise into a superset with the next one.
  • A hint under each exercise suggests your next target, such as adding a rep at the same weight.
  • Personal records are called out as you set them.
  • The session clock can be paused. If the app closes mid-workout, your session is still there when you come back.

Finishing

Tap Finish, then answer "How hard was that?" with one tap, from Easy to All out. That rating sharpens the load score for the session. Tap Skip if you'd rather not.

Exercises the app doesn't recognize are listed under Pending on the LIFT tab. Pick a muscle group for each one so it counts toward your muscle pace.

HEART RATE WITH APPLE WATCH

RepWarrior gets heart rate from Apple Watch through Apple Health. Rides, runs, walks, and other workouts your watch records come in automatically.

For lifting: start a Strength workout on your watch when you begin, and log your sets in RepWarrior as usual. When you finish, the two recordings merge into one session: your sets from the app, your heart rate from the watch.

After a few sessions like that, RepWarrior fits your strength load to your own heart-rate response instead of a general estimate. Settings → Calibration status shows how many sessions it has and how many it still needs.

Heart-rate zones come from your threshold heart rate, or you can type in zones exactly as a coach gave them.

TRAINING LOAD

Every session gets a load score, whether it was lifting, riding, running, or walking, so one number covers all of it. The LOAD tab shows today's picture and the last two weeks.

  • CTL (fitness) is your long-term average load, built over about six weeks.
  • ATL (fatigue) is your recent load, about the last week.
  • TSB (form) is fitness minus fatigue going into today. Positive means fresh, negative means you are carrying fatigue.
  • Days since hard counts from your last genuinely hard session.

Forgot to record a ride or a run? Log cardio on the LOAD tab takes the sport, time, distance, and average heart rate by hand.

SLEEP AND RECOVERY

The BODY tab shows last night's sleep with its stages, HRV and resting heart rate against your own 60 day baselines, and daily steps and energy.

A deload suggestion only appears when two or more of those measures agree that you need one. It is a suggestion; nothing changes unless you act on it.

TRENDS

The TRENDS tab charts your fitness curve, weekly load by sport, intensity mix by heart-rate zone, ride and walk efficiency, recovery, and the seasons of your training. Switch between 90 days, 6 months, 1 year, and all of your history at the top.

WEEKLY PLAN

Tap Build my week on Home to lay out your days: a template, an activity, easy cardio, a hard day, a long day, or rest. RepWarrior matches what you log against the plan and points out catch-ups on the right day. Without a plan, it coaches from the rhythm of your own training.

THE ARCADE

  • XP and levels come from training. XP never goes down.
  • LIFE is a row of pixel hearts on Home, filled by your recovery signals (form, HRV, sleep, resting heart rate) and spent by today's training. Tap it to see why it reads the way it does. You get more heart containers as you level up.
  • Encounters are creatures that show up when your training drifts, such as a lift that hasn't moved in a month or load that jumped faster than your body is used to. Each one says what it is weak to, and training that way beats it. The bestiary at the bottom of Home collects every creature you have met.
  • Badges fill the cabinet at the bottom of Home, and yesterday's wins are listed near the top.
  • Step quest earns a badge on every day you hit your step target.

Prefer a plain coach? Classic Mode in Settings hides the whole arcade layer. Nothing is lost or recalculated, and you can switch back any time. Reduce motion turns off blinking and sprite animation.

YOUR DATA

Your data lives on your iPhone and syncs through your own private iCloud. Settings → Your data has everything else:

  • Export my data saves a complete copy as one file.
  • Import from backup restores that file on any device.
  • Import Hevy export brings in your workout history from a Hevy CSV export.
  • Delete all my data removes everything from the device and your private iCloud.

WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

Health data is missing

Open Settings → Health access in the app to see what is actually arriving. Two switches matter: RepWarrior's read access in the Health app, and Apple Health sharing inside the companion app that came with your watch or band. RepWarrior can only read what that app writes into Health.

A lifting session has no heart rate

Heart rate for lifting comes from a Strength workout recorded on your Apple Watch at the same time. Without one, the session is still scored from your sets and reps in reserve.

Numbers look off after a big change

Your max heart rate, threshold, and FTP update from your own efforts. If you typed one in, it stays exactly as you set it. To hand it back, open Settings → Edit physiology and tap Let the app estimate these again.
