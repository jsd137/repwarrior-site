WHAT IT IS

RepWarrior™ is a strength logger and training-load coach for iPhone. It scores your lifting, riding, running, and walking into one picture of fitness and fatigue, coaches from your own training rhythm, and wraps it in an arcade layer that only ever awards.

  • Unlimited templates, last session's numbers pre-filled, reps in reserve, supersets, a rest timer, and a plate calculator
  • Fitness, fatigue, and form curves across every sport you do
  • Heart rate, HRV, sleep, and resting heart rate from Apple Watch through Apple Health
  • XP, a badge cabinet, a life bar of pixel hearts, and a 25 creature bestiary, all hideable with Classic Mode

Currently in beta on TestFlight. New here? The user guide walks through every screen.

YOUR DATA

Everything is processed on your device and stored there and in your private iCloud, which only you can access. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.
