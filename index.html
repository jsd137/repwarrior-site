WHAT IT IS

RepWarrior is a strength logger and training-load coach for iPhone. It scores your lifting, riding, and walking into one honest picture of fitness and fatigue, coaches from your own training rhythm, and wraps the whole thing in an arcade layer that only ever awards. No accounts, no server, no ads.

Currently in beta on TestFlight.

YOUR DATA

Everything is processed on your device and stored there and in your private iCloud, which only you can access. Nothing is ever sold, shared, or sent to us.
