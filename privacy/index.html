REPWARRIOR

PRIVACY POLICY

THE SHORT VERSION

Your data stays yours. On your device and in your private iCloud. RepWarrior™ has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

WHAT THE APP READS

With your permission, RepWarrior reads your workout, heart-rate, sleep, HRV, resting heart-rate, step, and energy data from Apple Health to compute training load and recovery guidance on your device. If you connect a Bluetooth heart-rate monitor, its readings are processed the same way. Both permissions are optional; the app is fully functional without them.

WHERE IT LIVES

Your data is stored on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. Your data is never sold, shared, or transmitted to the developer or any third party. The app's only network use is your own private iCloud sync.

YOUR CONTROLS

  • Revoke Health access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • Export a complete copy of your data from the app (Settings → Your data).
  • Delete all app data from the app itself, or by deleting the app. iCloud data can be removed in Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage.

CHANGES

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear on this page before it takes effect. Effective date: July 26, 2026.
